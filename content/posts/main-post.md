## Design
### Needfinding
We did a mixture of stream of consciousness, worst idea, and talking to someone outside of our group to generate ideas. We found out that it would be beneficial to have averages for both dishes and stations, being completely anonymous probably wasn't good if we wanted a following page too, favoriting stuff to be notified would be nice, adding nutrition info on top of ratings helps people who just look for food, allow recipe/food tips could help with making dhall a better experience too, and that ensuring it's a grinnell student would be good to prevent bot/spam.

### Ideation
We interviewd one person each, we found out that some things we wanted we could potentially do without for early on development, primarily adding recipes, photos uploaded by students, and text reviews over just a number. They would be beneficial but the focus is to make dhall a better experience not necessarily social aspect, though people use rating apps to also see what stuff they're friends are up to so it would help promote user engagment. We want logging in to be clear and also want to probably prompt users when we add written reviews about what they should write about. Filtering reviews by recency and potentially allowing you to like reviews to promote good ones from being seen easily will make it easier for students to know trends and recent changes.

### Prototyping
The questions we intended on answering with our prototypes are: How easy and inutitive is it to review or log an item? How easy and intuititve is it to find the information you want? Does each page on the app show what you would expect that page to do? I focused on the second question "How easy and intuititve is it to find the information you want?" by making a paper prototype of our homescreen where all the days dishes would be, where you can switch dates and meal times, and where you can see the reviews for each station and inspect. It was designed to be interactive so anything that you can click on I created the submenus and updates on the menu on sticky notes or on a seperate "screen" to show you moving onto another screen so people could interact with it like it was a functioining version and I did the "thinking" of the app. I found that people still really wanted a way to a way to add photos to the dishes and would appreciate it even without reviewing, they also would still desire to add tips to many sections or as separate area to allow users to give other students advice on combos/hacks, and there was a desire to make nutrition easier to acces since it was behind a few clicks with a separate view or section for it potentially being beneficial.
